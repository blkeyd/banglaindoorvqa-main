HT-6 option review

This is a test sheet without pictures. It cannot be used for the review.

Bangla only: this set is never shown in English, so no English names are listed.

    Enter confirms this set and opens the next one. ← and → move without confirming.

    Each set has a picture, one right answer and three wrong answers. A model will see the picture and the four names and must pick one. You check that none of the three wrong answers is also right.

    Take a wrong answer out in two cases only:

    • It is also a right name for the main thing in the picture.
    • The thing it names is also a main object of the picture: as large and as central as the right answer.

    Leave it in when the thing is not in the picture, or is small, at the edge or in the background. When you take one out, the next candidate appears in its place. Check that one too.

    Type your initials first.

    This file holds photos of homes with their names. Keep it on your own computer and do not share it. Delete it after you upload the log.

    Rules for this review

    • The first row is the right answer. Never change it.
    • Take a wrong answer out if it is also a right name for the main thing in the picture, in Bangla or in English.
    • Take a wrong answer out if the thing it names is also a main object of the picture: as large and as central as the right answer.
    • Leave a wrong answer in if the thing is absent, small, at the edge or in the background.
    • Leave a wrong answer in if the two names are only close in meaning in general. Decide from this picture.
    • The names are frozen. Do not judge whether a name is the best word, and do not suggest another one.
    • When a new option appears, check it the same way.
    • If the right answer itself looks wrong, or the picture is unclear, tick the box, write a note, and go on.
    • Work alone. Do not look at any model answer while you review.
